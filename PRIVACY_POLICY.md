# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 21, 2026

## Introduction

Auxo ("the App") is a strength training application that helps users create and execute workout routines. This Privacy Policy explains how user data is handled.

## Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers. All data created within the App (workouts, exercises, and settings) is stored exclusively on the user's device using a local SQLite database.

The App does not intentionally collect:

- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics or crash reports
- Advertising identifiers
- Biometric or health information

The App requires the following permissions solely for local functionality:

- **Notifications**: To alert users when exercise rest periods end, including when the App is running in the background.
- **Audio**: To play timer and workout sounds.

No data obtained through these permissions is transmitted outside the device by the App.

## Data Storage

All user data is stored locally on the device. No cloud services, external servers, or third-party databases are used by the App. Users may delete all App data at any time by clearing the App storage or uninstalling the App.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external data collection services.

Please note that the Google Play Store or the Android operating system may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
